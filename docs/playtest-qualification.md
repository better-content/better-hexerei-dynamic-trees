## Primary shipped gameplay promise in this source

This addon supplies Dynamic Trees species for Hexerei **mahogany, willow, and witch hazel** (`src/main/resources/trees/better_hexerei_dynamic_trees/{species,families,leaves_properties}/`). The species use Hexerei primitive logs, leaves, and saplings; dynamic willow specifies Hexerei hanging vine blocks. `world_gen/default.json` splices mahogany into jungle/bamboo jungle, willow into `#forge:is_swamp`, and witch hazel into birch forest without overriding biome tree density or chance. `world_gen/feature_cancellers.json` and `HexereiTreeFeatureCanceller.java` cancel matching Hexerei static tree features in those selected biomes to avoid double-spawning. They do not prove actual trees generate or static features disappear in terrain. This is compatibility/replacement behavior, **not** a new crafting or GUI system.

**Acquisition references:** generated `data/better_hexerei_dynamic_trees/loot_tables/blocks/{mahogany,willow,witch_hazel}_leaves.json` contain Dynamic Trees seed-item drops (with seasonal/Fortune conditions), and `loot_tables/trees/voluntary/{mahogany,willow,witch_hazel}.json` contain voluntary seed drops; `data/dynamictrees/tags/items/seeds.json` lists all three addon seeds. `src/main/resources/trees/.../species/*.json` associates them with `hexerei:*_sapling`; generated blockstates/models and `assets/.../lang/en_us.json` identify sapling/seed/branch entries. These are **source references**, not a demonstrated survival acquisition route or drop-frequency guarantee. No recipe JSON or crafting entry point exists in this repository; pack-wide recipe-root and ingredient availability must be checked in the full pack, including dependencies' recipes and loot. Do not silently substitute creative inventory as proof of survival access.
